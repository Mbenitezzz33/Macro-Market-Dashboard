# Macroeconomic Dashboard

An Excel and Power BI dashboard that is designed to evaluate the macroeconomic environment through a structured economic regime framework. The project combines economic data analysis, VBA automation, and interactive Power BI reporting to transform raw economic indicators into actionable insights.

## Project Overview

This project was developed to create a repeatable framework for evaluating the health and direction of the U.S. economy. Rather than focusing solely on individual indicators, the dashboard groups economic data into five key categories and classifies each area using standardized market checks.

The framework evaluates:

- Economic Growth
- Labor Market
- Inflation
- Interest Rates
- Credit Conditions

Each category is analyzed using current data, prior-period comparisons, trend measurements, and classification logic to generate a concise macroeconomic assessment.

## Economic Framework

### Economic Growth

Measures overall economic activity using:

- GDP Growth
- ISM Manufacturing PMI
- ISM Services PMI
- Industrial Production

### Labor Market

Measures employment strength using:

- Nonfarm Payrolls
- Unemployment Rate
- Labor Force Participation Rate
- Wage Growth

### Inflation

Measures price stability using:

- CPI
- Core CPI
- PCE
- Core PCE

### Interest Rates

Evaluates monetary policy conditions using:

- Federal Funds Rate
- 2-Year Treasury Yield
- 10-Year Treasury Yield
- Yield Curve Spread

### Credit Conditions

Evaluates financial market liquidity using:

- High Yield Option-Adjusted Spread
- Investment Grade Option-Adjusted Spread

---

## Classification Methodology

The dashboard classifies indicators using a combination of:

- Direction (Growing / Contracting)
- Rate of Change (Accelerating / Decelerating / Stable)
- Trend Duration
- Policy Conditions
- Credit Market Conditions

These classifications are combined to generate an overall macroeconomic regime and risk assessment.

### Example Outputs

- Expansion
- Recovery
- Contraction
- Healthy but Slowing
- High and Rising Inflation
- Restrictive and Stable
- Loose and Stable
